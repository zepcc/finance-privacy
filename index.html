Private finance tool: privacy and terms

Last updated: 1 November 2026

What this is

A private, single-user tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It reads balance and transaction information from the owner's own business bank account, through the open banking service of Enable Banking, so the owner can do their company bookkeeping. It is not a public service and has no other users.

Privacy

  • Data accessed: account balances and transactions of the owner's own accounts only, with the owner's consent given in the bank's own app.
  • Access type: account information only. The tool never initiates payments or changes anything at the bank.
  • Storage: the data is stored only on the owner's computer. It is not shared with anyone, sold, published or used for advertising or profiling.
  • Credentials: the tool never sees or stores the owner's bank login. Access is approved in the bank's app and expires automatically, and the owner can withdraw it at any time through the bank or Enable Banking.
  • Retention: kept only as long as the owner's accounting records require.

Terms

  • The tool is for the owner's private use only and is not offered to third parties.
  • It is provided as is, without any warranty. Figures it produces are working notes, not accounting or tax filings.
